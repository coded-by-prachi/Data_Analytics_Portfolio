## 1. Railway Train Route and Enquiry Analytics System

**Tools:** Python, Pandas

An analytics project focused on cleaning and analyzing railway train and station data to understand routes, station traffic, route sequences, and source-to-destination train availability.

### Key Work
- Cleaned and transformed railway train and station data.
- Handled inconsistent station names and time-related data.
- Analyzed train routes and station sequences.
- Identified high-traffic railway stations.
- Built a source-to-destination train enquiry function.
- Handled overnight journey durations correctly.
- Validated station and route information during analysis.

### Outcome
Developed an analytical enquiry system that can identify direct trains between source and destination stations along with journey duration.
